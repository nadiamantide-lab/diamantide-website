THE BAND

DIAMANTIDE IS
MORE THAN A SOUND.

Formed in the San Francisco Bay Area, Diamantide brings together the precision of progressive metal, the force of heavy riffs, and an unmistakably melodic heart.

Our songs explore identity, fear, love, loss, and resilience — stories told with music that can be intimate one moment and thunderous the next.

From the compositions to the lyrics and vocal melodies, every arrangement is built to connect. Pronounced: Dee-ah-MAHN-tee-deh
